# Visuels de connexion

- `symbiose-logo.png` : logo transparent fourni par l’utilisateur, copié sans modification.
- `auth-forest.png` : fond retouché avec l’outil intégré `image_gen`, à partir de la maquette « Connexion en symbiose dans la forêt.png ». Le formulaire est rendu en HTML par `AuthPage.vue`.

Consigne de la première retouche :

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached forest login page mockup. Produce ONLY the background image for this website. Remove the entire central glass login card, its border, ALL text, the logo, fields, buttons and every form element; reconstruct the natural forest that was behind it with continuous tree trunks, dark green vegetation, moss and forest floor. Preserve as faithfully as possible the surrounding landscape, warm sunbeams in the upper left, deer at left, mossy foreground and ferns. Preserve the subtle translucent curved ribbon and green topographic lines/location marker at the far right. Keep the center dark green and calm for overlaying a real HTML form later. Wide landscape 16:9, photorealistic, high quality. Absolutely no text, no logo, no panel, no UI controls, no frame.

Le fond final `auth-forest.png` a ensuite été retouché avec l’outil intégré `image_gen` pour retirer le liseré et la carte à droite, à la demande de l’utilisateur. Consigne exacte :

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied forest background image. Remove ONLY the artificial overlays on the right side: the entire broad translucent curved ribbon and its thin light borders in the upper right, the entire green topographic map contour line graphic in the middle right, and the bright green location pin. Seamlessly replace those graphic areas with natural forest, tree trunks, leaves and ferns matching the surrounding photograph. Preserve the forest composition, lighting, warm sunbeams at upper left, the deer on the left, mossy foreground, tree trunks and dark green center as faithfully as possible. Keep the same landscape dimensions and framing. The final result must be a purely natural photorealistic forest with no map, no marker, no lines, no ribbon, no graphic overlays, no text or logo.
